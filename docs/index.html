Advent Together

An advent calendar your whole group fills together

Everyone claims a door and hides a surprise behind it: a message, a joke, a song, a recipe, an old photo. One door opens each morning for the whole group. Free, no sign-up, no app.

Open Advent Together

How it works

  1. Make a calendar (Christmas advent, a 12-day countdown, or any countdown up to 31 days) and share the link in your group chat.
  2. Friends open the link, type a name and claim a door. What they hide stays secret until its day.
  3. One door opens each day at midnight in the calendar’s time zone, showing who it’s from. Empty doors open with a small surprise from us.
