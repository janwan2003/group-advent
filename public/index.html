Questions

Who can see what, and when?

Before a door opens, the group only sees that it’s taken. What’s behind it, and who filled it, appear when the door opens at midnight in the calendar’s time zone. The person who filled a door can always see and edit their own until then.

What if nobody fills a door?

It still opens, with a small surprise from us: a joke, a question for the group or a tiny challenge. The calendar never has a blank day.

Does everyone need to fill a door?

No. Opening the doors takes one tap. Filling one is optional, and some people fill several.

What can I put behind a door?

A message, a joke, a song or video link, a recipe, a memory, a challenge for the group, and one photo.

Can I start late?

Yes. Doors that already opened show their surprise straight away, and everyone can still fill the doors that haven’t opened yet.

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Free, no account, no email, no ads. You join a calendar with a name. Your browser remembers you, and you can copy a personal link to use it on another device.
