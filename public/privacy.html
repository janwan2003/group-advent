Advent Together

Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Advent Together is built to need almost nothing from you. There are no accounts, no email addresses, no ads and no tracking cookies.

What we store

  • The calendar: its name, dates, number of doors and time zone.
  • Members: the name each person types when joining, and a random key that your browser keeps so it remembers you. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of that key.
  • Doors: what each person hides behind a door (text, a link and optionally one photo, resized in your browser before upload) and the reactions to it.
  • Abuse protection: a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address with a request counter, kept for at most an hour, so one visitor cannot overload the service.

Who can see it

Anyone with a calendar’s link can see who has joined it and every door that has already opened, including who filled it. A door’s content stays hidden from everyone except the person who filled it until the door opens. Calendar pages are marked so search engines do not index them. Don’t put anything behind a door that you wouldn’t share with the whole group.

How long we keep it

Each calendar, with all its doors, photos and members, is deleted automatically 60 days after its last door opens. The person who made a calendar can delete it at any time. Anyone can leave a calendar, which removes their name and their doors that haven’t opened yet.

Where it lives

The site and its data are hosted on Cloudflare. We do not sell or share data with anyone else, and we do not use analytics beyond aggregate counts (for example, the number of calendars).

Contact

Questions or a removal request: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/group-advent.
